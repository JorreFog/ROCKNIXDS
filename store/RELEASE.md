The ROCKNIXDS Store: games and apps for ROCKNIXDS, installed and updated from the menu.

This is a release of the Store itself. Installed Stores update themselves from it (the Store's Installed tab, or
`/storage/.config/rocknixds/store/rocknixds-store update store` over ssh). ROCKNIXDS's installer puts the Store on the
handheld with ROCKNIXDS; see [store/README.md](https://github.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/blob/main/store/README.md).

`rocknixds-store-<version>-aarch64.tar.gz` holds the app, its package manager, the catalog as it was at this release
and the device scripts; the `.sha256` beside it is checked before anything is installed.
