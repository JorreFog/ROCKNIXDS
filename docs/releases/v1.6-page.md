## 🔺 3× internal resolution

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/main/docs/img/1.6/3x-resolution.png" width="880" alt="One 3D scene, the same crop at 1x (256x192, the DS), 2x (512x384, ROCKNIXDS 1.5) and 3x (768x576, new in 1.6): the stair-stepped polygon edges of 1x and 2x are smooth at 3x">
</p>

Gengis Engine can draw a game's 3D at 768×576 and supersample it into the panel: smoother polygon edges and 2.25
samples per pixel of texture detail.

## 🏦 ROCKNIXDS Bank & Trade

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/main/docs/img/1.6/bank-trade.png" width="880" alt="Bank & Trade: two handhelds trading a Pokémon over Wi-Fi; a bank of 40 boxes, reads your game saves, PKHeX legality checks, trades over Wi-Fi, trade evolutions">
</p>

A Pokémon bank, legality checker and trading app on both screens, built on PKHeX.Core. Move Pokémon between your
games through the bank, check them with PKHeX's legality analysis, and trade between two handhelds over Wi-Fi.
**Ports > ROCKNIXDS Bank.**

## 🧟 Döda Kvarter

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/main/docs/img/1.6/doda-kvarter.png" width="880" alt="Döda Kvarter in 90s box-art style: the title screen on a beige CRT TV with a VHS PLAY overlay, the logo in red and chrome, a NYTT SPEL! sticker, a Windows 95 window listing the game's features, and the survivor facing the game's zombies and eight folklore bosses on a snowy street">
</p>

A zombie roguelike for both screens, set in a Swedish suburb at night: rounds that never end, kronor for every hit,
the Mystery Box, perks and Pack-a-Punch, eight bosses from Swedish folklore, and a new town every run. It draws every
pixel itself, at 60 frames a second. **Ports > Döda Kvarter.**
