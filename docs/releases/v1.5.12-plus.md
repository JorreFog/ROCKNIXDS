# ROCKNIXDS 1.5.12 for the RG DS Plus
**Cartridge labels show the whole label.** With ScreenScraper's cartridge art (a photo of the whole card), ROCKNIXDS
Pixel zoomed in on the middle of the label: Mario Kart DS read "MARIOKAR", and the logos lost their sides. The
cartridges now show the label's full width: the whole logo, the age rating and the Nintendo seal. Tested on an RG DS
Plus with six DS games scraped from ScreenScraper.

> **RG DS (non-Plus) owners:** your release and image are on **[v1.5.12](https://github.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/releases/tag/v1.5.12)**.

## Install or upgrade

From 1.5: **Updates & downloads > ROCKNIXDS** in the menu. New SD card: the image attached below, see the
[README's Get started](https://github.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS#get-started).
