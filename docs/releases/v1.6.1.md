## Install or upgrade

From 1.5: **Updates & downloads > ROCKNIXDS** in the menu. New SD card: the image attached below, see the
[README's Get started](https://github.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS#get-started). From anything else, ssh into the handheld
as `root` (password `rocknix`) and run:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/main/install.sh | sh
```

ROCKNIXDS Bank & Trade is a separate install (below). Döda Kvarter comes with the update.

## 🔺 3× internal resolution

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/v1.6.1/1.6%20artwork/05-3x-resolution.png" width="880" alt="One 3D scene, the same crop at 1x (256x192, the DS), 2x (512x384, ROCKNIXDS 1.5) and 3x (768x576, new in 1.6): the stair-stepped polygon edges of 1x and 2x are smooth at 3x">
</p>

Gengis Engine can draw a game's 3D at three times the DS's size and bring it down to the 2× picture: smoother polygon
edges, the same layout. **Nintendo DS settings (or a game's) > 3D resolution: 3x.** It costs CPU, so 3x runs on the
performance power profile unless the game has its own profile set (Pokémon HeartGold walking held 60 fps at 3x on
a Plus in the 3x test build). DraStic's own renderer ignores the setting.

## 🏦 ROCKNIXDS Bank & Trade

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/v1.6.1/1.6%20artwork/06-bank-trade.png" width="880" alt="Bank & Trade: two handhelds trading a Pokémon over Wi-Fi; a bank of 40 boxes, reads your game saves, PKHeX legality checks, trades over Wi-Fi, trade evolutions">
</p>

A Pokémon bank, legality checker and trading app on both screens, built on PKHeX.Core: it reads your game saves
(DraStic's, melonDS's, RetroArch's), stores Pokémon in 40 boxes of its own, moves them between games through the bank
(up a generation as Pal Park and Poké Transfer did, never back down), checks every one with PKHeX's legality
analysis, and trades between two handhelds over Wi-Fi: open lobbies that every handheld on the network sees, or a
private room joined with a code. Trade evolutions happen on arrival. Over ssh:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/main/bank/device/install-bank.sh | sh
```

It gets **a tile of its own on the home page** (*Bank & Trade*). Close the game whose save you open: emulators write
their own copy of the save when they quit. The [bank's README](https://github.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/tree/main/bank)
has the rest. Tested on an RG DS Plus with Black 2, HeartGold and generated saves of every generation, and with two
instances trading over Wi-Fi.

## 🧟 Döda Kvarter

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/v1.6.1/1.6%20artwork/07-doda-kvarter.png" width="880" alt="Döda Kvarter in 90s box-art style: the title screen on a beige CRT TV with a VHS PLAY overlay, the logo in red and chrome, a NYTT SPEL! sticker, a Windows 95 window listing the game's features, and the survivor facing the game's zombies and eight folklore bosses on a snowy street">
</p>

A zombie roguelike made for both screens, set in a Swedish suburb at night: rounds that never end, kronor for every
hit, barricades into new districts, the Mystery Box (Lådan), perks as Swedish food, Pack-a-Punch (Smedjan), eight
bosses from Swedish folklore, and a new town every run; *Today's town* is the same for everyone on the same day. It
draws every pixel itself on the bare panels at 60 frames a second, and it has **a tile of its own on the home page**.
Settings > Buttons sets the layout and a button for every action. On the RG DS it draws 2× pixels, on the Plus 3×.

**New in 1.6.1, Döda Kvarter 0.2:**
- **Difficulty: Easy, Medium, Hard** (Settings > Difficulty; a run takes it when it starts). The default is
  **Medium**: the dead (and the bosses) hit 30% softer, have 20% less health, come 15% fewer to a round, and turn into
  runners and sprinters more slowly. **Easy** goes further (55%, 40%, 30% off). **Hard** is 0.1's game. The high
  score list marks each run E, M or H.
- **Lock on: hold R2.** While you hold it, your aim stays locked on the nearest zombie in sight, with a red marker
  round it; let go and the aim is free again. Let go and hold it again at once to lock on the next nearest. When the
  one you're locked on falls, the aim moves to the nearest by itself. The right stick or a touch on the top screen
  still aims where you point.
- **The buttons that moved**, to free R2: the grenade is on **L2**, and the bag is all on **SELECT**: a tap picks the
  next item, holding it uses it (or tap the bag on the bottom screen). Settings > Buttons changes any of it.
- **The game updates itself.** A new Döda Kvarter doesn't need a ROCKNIXDS update: the title says when there is one
  (with Wi-Fi on), and **Settings > Game updates** downloads it, checks it, puts it in place and starts it. High
  scores and settings stay. A ROCKNIXDS update never puts an older game back over a newer one.

## 🛟 A stuck game never needs a restart again

On 2026-10-05 a Plus froze right after Pokémon Black 2 loaded its resume state: the game stopped drawing and nothing
ended it; the handheld had to be restarted. Now:
- **The cause is found and fixed.** DraStic starts four of its helper threads before it sets up the locks they wait
  on; a helper that was quick waited on a lock DraStic then wiped, and nothing ever woke it: a game that showed no
  frame, or stopped at its first 3D frame. It struck 4 of 25 HeartGold starts on a Plus. SuperDrastic now holds
  those threads at the door until their locks exist: 0 of 40 starts since, and 0 of 6 with the race forced (4 of 4
  froze without the fix).
- **5 s without a picture** (outside DraStic's own menu and the in-game menu): a red card says *The game stopped
  responding*. The exit hotkey then takes you to the menu at once, and the log records what every part of the game
  was doing.
- **20 s without a picture**: the game is closed, and the menu says why.
- **The exit hotkey always ends the game.** A second press, or a game that doesn't take the save within 3 seconds,
  quits at once (without a resume state).
- **A game frozen solid** (even its display engine stuck) is ended from outside after 15 s, with the same message.

All of it checked on a Plus (`docs/1.6-prep/stall-checks.sh`: 7 of 7).

## 🎮 The in-game menu

Clicking the left stick (L3) during a game opens a menu in ROCKNIXDS Pixel's look instead of DraStic's old one:
Resume; Save and Load, with eight slots, a picture in each, and *Undo last load*; Quick settings (volume, brightness,
a live microphone meter and *Blow*); DraStic's own menu; Quit. The top screen shows the paused game, this session's
play time, the last save and your latest achievements. Beta testers have had it since 1.5.13 beta 1; since then a
selected empty slot on the Load page is readable and dates have no double space.

## ⏱️ Back to the menu in about a second, and it looks clean

Quitting a DS game no longer restarts EmulationStation. The menu stays up during the game and is back about a second
after you quit, whatever the size of your library (measured on a Plus: about 1.7 s). Between the menu and the game
the screens are plain black: no terminal cursor, and no leftover frame of the start animation. The first button
press after a game is no longer lost (sway had its display back but no input devices until something made it look;
6 of 9 first presses were ignored, now 7 of 7 registered). `/storage/.config/drastic/dsflip/fast-switch off` (over
ssh) goes back to the old way.

## 🖼️ The menu: tiles, cartridges, pictures

- **Döda Kvarter and Bank & Trade have tiles of their own** on ROCKNIXDS Pixel's home page, with pixel icons of
  their own: A on the tile starts the app.
- **No cartridge is ever empty.** ROCKNIX's pictures for its Tools (File Manager, the emulators' own menus...) are
  SVG files the menu couldn't read, so every cartridge in Tools was blank: they show now. An entry with no picture at
  all (the Music Player, a game nobody scraped) shows its system's icon on a plain label.
- The *game stopped responding* notice fits its box; *Tools* and *Music Player* no longer carry an empty-box glyph
  before "System"; a game never started says *Not played yet* on the home card; a list of one shows no neighbours.

## 🗂️ A fresh card opens on the DS

With no DS games yet, EmulationStation used to drop the DS. The menu then opened on Music Player and two empty
collections, and their placeholder hung the ready screen when tapped. Now the DS stays listed and says **No games yet
/ Copy .nds files to roms/nds**; collections say *Nothing here yet*; collection names are capitalised on the shelf.

## ⚙️ The best settings are the defaults

*Auto* is the best choice everywhere: the **Gengis Engine** renderer (the same picture as DraStic's renderer with
about 12% less CPU work), the DS's own texture filter, the balanced power profile, threaded 3D. A player who picked
*DraStic* keeps it.

## 🏆 RetroAchievements without a connection

A login that gets no answer (no Wi-Fi yet, a DNS hiccup, a busy server) no longer deletes your saved login. It is
tried again in the background: after 15 s, 30 s and a minute, then every 2 minutes. You see one pop-up, *no
connection*, instead of *login failed* with nothing after it. (Built and read against the server library's code;
not exercised on a handheld with the routes blocked.)

## 🧠 The menu stays light

On 2026-10-05 EmulationStation grew to 713 MB on a Plus until the system closed it. With a menu open over a ROCKNIXDS
Pixel game list while its pictures still loaded, the theme could ask for the same pictures again on every frame and
keep every copy; that is fixed, and 1.6 makes sure nothing like it can take the handheld down again: the Pixel
theme's picture cache has a size limit, image memory goes back to the system when it is freed, the menu's memory is
written to `es-mem.log` as it changes, past half the handheld's memory with no game running the menu restarts by
itself, and two quick launch requests start the game once, not twice. (The watch and the launch guard were built and
tested on a PC; the handheld's long-run numbers are still to come.)

## 🔤 An easier pixel font

In ROCKNIXDS Pixel's font, **5** no longer looks like **S**, **2** and **Z** are told apart, and **B** no longer
reads as **G** (issue #34).

## 🧩 From your reports

- **#36, #37:** the DS settings disappeared, and Tools and Music Player showed up in the system settings, when the
  options file was left broken. The file is now checked before it is replaced, and repaired or rebuilt from ROCKNIX's.
- **#31:** zipped DS games get their RetroAchievements id from the background job too.
- **#26:** the in-game menu's *Blow* presses the fake microphone however `drastic.cfg` binds it. A real blow is still
  being looked into: see *Known issues*.
- **#32, #34, #35:** *View Game Media*, the Save State Manager and *Manual Scrape* fit on the bottom screen instead of
  spreading over both. A picture opens whole; L/R zoom and the D-pad moves it.
- **#44:** *UI settings > ROM icon on cartridges (DS)* (ROCKNIXDS Pixel, off by default) shows a DS game's own icon,
  from its ROM, on the bottom screen's cartridges instead of the box art. Zipped games too.
- **#27:** ROCKNIXDS Pixel's game list takes touch: drag along its progress bar to scroll (the current letter rides
  above it) or tap the bar to jump, and tap the ◀ ▶ arrows by the names at the top.
- **#43:** not a bug: ROCKNIX turns front-end music on but ships no music. Copy `.mp3`/`.ogg` files to `roms/music`.

## 📶 Online play is not in 1.6

The 1.5.13 test build had a *wfc dns* option for Nintendo WFC replacement servers. It doesn't get past the game's own
Wi-Fi setup yet, so 1.6 neither shows it nor runs it. The work continues separately.

## Known issues

- **A real blow into the microphone may do nothing** (issue #26): the speaker reaches the microphone as loud as a
  blow, and the echo gate then holds its bar above any blow. The in-game menu's *Blow* is the way around it for now.
- **Japanese and Chinese game names** show as empty boxes in the in-game menu (the pixel font has no such letters).
  Bank & Trade shows them (it has a fallback font).
- **RetroAchievements:** softcore only.
- **Heavy stretches at 2× can still drop frames** in some 3D games; 3× more so.
- **Front-end music** plays only files you add: copy `.mp3` or `.ogg` files to `roms` > `music`.
