**The first stable ROCKNIXDS for the Anbernic RG DS Plus, and it's the big one.** ROCKNIXDS 1.5 gives the Plus a
frontend of its own: **ROCKNIXDS Pixel**, a pixel-art menu across both 1024×768 panels, drawn by a brand-new engine
built into EmulationStation. Your games are cartridges with their real label art, the box cover floats on the top
screen, and when you press A the cartridge slides into the console with a *click*. Under it: DS games at full speed
on the Plus, games that pick up where you quit, power profiles, a CPU clock that learns each game, art and
RetroAchievements fetched by the handheld itself, and updates straight from the menu.

> **RG DS (non-Plus) owners:** your release is **[v1.5](https://github.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/releases/tag/v1.5)**.
> The install command and the menu's updater pick the right one for you.

## Install or upgrade

ssh into the RG DS Plus as `root` (password `rocknix`) and run the same command as on the RG DS:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/main/install.sh | sh
```

That's all: the installer sees it's on a Plus, installs this release, upgrades any earlier ROCKNIXDS in place, and backs up
everything it replaces. From 1.5 on you never need the command again: **Updates & downloads > ROCKNIXDS** in the menu
checks for, downloads and installs updates. Built for ROCKNIX 20260901.

## 🚀 Made for the RG DS Plus

Everything is sized from the panels: two 1024×768 panels, a 3072×768 canvas for the dual-screen themes, the boot
splash and the menus. And DS games run at full speed:
- DraStic's emulation thread gets **a CPU of its own**, and its 3D helper threads one per remaining core, busiest
  first. Pokémon Black 2 went from 1.7–2.6 repeated frames a second to **about one every 12 s**, Dragon Quest
  Monsters from 3.6 to **0.2**.
- Both panels' frames are committed early enough for the bottom panel, whose vblank is 6.8 ms before the top's.
- PipeWire stays at **48 kHz**, the only rate the Plus's speaker amp runs: forcing DraStic's 44.1 kHz played
  everything 7% fast and made the audio stutter.
- A DS session runs on the performance governor whatever ROCKNIX's setting (the Plus nightly defaults to ondemand,
  under which the clock control switched itself off), and the GPU governor watcher keeps running during the game.
- DraStic's *threaded 3d* is switched on where it was never set.
- The volume indicator works in the menus, and SuperDrastic draws its own while a game runs.

A whole new theme, and a whole new engine to draw it, at 1.6× on the Plus's panels: ROCKNIXDS Pixel isn't an XML skin, it's its own renderer,
views, animations and input inside the patched EmulationStation. It comes in two flavours:
**Pixel Light** (your theme after installing or updating to 1.5) and **Pixel Dark** (one switch away in the theme
menu).

**Home**
- A **system shelf** on the bottom screen that glides like a conveyor belt: icons slide in from the edge, never jump.
- **31 hand-drawn pixel-art icons** with their own colour for every system, from the DS and the Game Boys to arcade,
  ports, music and video.
- The top screen shows the system: its games, how many you've played, your total play time and the **last game you
  played**. Press **X** to jump straight back into it.

**Game list**
- Your games are **DS cartridges wearing their real card's label art**, edge to edge in the cartridge's label window.
  The scan's white header and code strip are trimmed away automatically; a game without a card scan wears its box
  cover until it has one.
- On the top screen the **box cover floats** above its shadow, with a **shine that sweeps across it** every few
  seconds.
- Next to it: the title (balanced nicely over two lines), genre and year, **plays, last played and time played**, and
  your **RetroAchievements progress**.
- The title bubble has a fixed height, so long names never push the layout around.

**Starting a game**
- The cartridge **lifts, slides and clicks into the console** before the game starts.

**Sound and look**
- **Chiptune menu sounds** for moving, opening, going back, favouriting and the cartridge's click, switched on for you
  (*Sound settings > Enable navigation sounds*).
- A **boot splash** in the same pixel look, and the **Pixelify Sans** pixel font everywhere.
- Light and dark palettes, dithered pixel boxes, and an idle menu that only draws when something moves (it saves
  battery).

**Controls:** L/R or the d-pad move, **A** opens, **B** goes back, **X** resumes the system's last game, **Y**
favourites the system or the game. Tap or swipe the bottom screen too.

## ⏯️ Pick up where you left off

The exit hotkey now **saves your place and quits**. The next time you start that game, it resumes right there, once.
- The save is a savestate of its own: it never touches your slots.
- A resume state older than the game's own save file is dropped, so it never undoes in-game saving.
- Per system or per game: *resume on quit* (on by default). With it off, the hotkey quits as before.

## 🔋 Power profiles

*Game settings > Per system advanced configuration > Nintendo DS > Power profile*, or per game (hold A on it >
*Advanced game options*):

| Profile | CPU | Queue |
|---|---|---|
| **Balanced** (default) | up to 1416 MHz | one more frame (+17 ms input latency); a full queue holds DraStic a moment instead of dropping a frame |
| **Performance** | up to 1992 MHz | the shortest, for the lowest latency |
| **Battery saver** | 1104–1416 MHz on the Plus (its frames cost more) | three frames (+33 ms) |

Balanced and performance were measured on the RG DS (Pokémon Black 2 at 2×: balanced gave 0.07 hitches/s and no
dropped frames); the Plus's battery saver starts at 1104 MHz because a fixed 1104 overran its heavy frames.

## 🧠 A CPU clock that learns each game

- Clocks that dropped frames are **remembered per game** and skipped from the start of the next session, instead of
  being found again by dropping frames. Only drops that really come from the CPU count.
- The clock control counts **all of DraStic's threads**, not just the emulation thread.
- **No more stutter storms:** a pacing bug that could leave every frame committed late, and a minute of dropped
  frames with it, is fixed.

## 🖼️ Art and RetroAchievements, fetched by the handheld

- Every time the menu opens, a background job at idle priority **scrapes the games missing a 3D box, screenshot or
  cartridge**. No PC, no scraper account.
- Retail art first (before demos, kiosks and hacks), in the ROM's own region.
- A game with no match is tried again a week later; an offline handheld tries again the next time the menu opens.
- The **RetroAchievements strip** of every game played since its strip was drawn is redrawn; the game you just quit
  is redrawn while the menu starts, so its progress bar already shows what you unlocked.
- New games get their RetroAchievements ID on the device, with the same hash ES uses.

## ⬆️ Updates from the menu

- **Updates & downloads > ROCKNIXDS** shows the installed version and channel (**stable** releases or **beta**), and
  checks for and installs updates.
- A timer checks every 6 hours and shows a notification once per new update (switch it off there).
- On a Plus, stable is the newest `-plus` release and beta is the `plus-beta` branch, at exactly the version the
  check found: **never an RG DS build**. Plus beta testers stay on the beta channel unless they switch to stable.
- The menu tells you if an update fails. ROCKNIX's own OS updates are hidden: a new ROCKNIX can need a new ROCKNIXDS.

## 🛡️ A safer menu

Settings that break ROCKNIXDS are gone from the menus: the dual-screen layout options, CPU/GPU governors, GPU driver,
video mode and rotation, DTB overlays, developer options, factory reset, the emulator-config reset, and the DS
system's emulator choice. (Developers: `touch /storage/.config/rocknixds/unlocked` and restart the menu.)

## ✨ And also

- The startup game and the update pill in the menu are fixed.
- **The menu is back ~0.5 s sooner** after a DS game.
- *Launch this game at startup* runs once per boot instead of after every quit.
- Short option values for *resume on quit* and *power profile*, so the option names fit.
- The DraStic engine is its own project now: **[SuperDrastic](https://github.com/JorreFog/SuperDrastic)**, for any
  Linux firmware. This release installs SuperDrastic 0.3.0-beta.2.
- Installer: a smoother uninstall (settings you changed since are kept, `rocknixds.*` keys removed, files that weren't
  there before the install are removed instead of restored), and host tests for the installer, updater, media
  matching and auto-scrape on GitHub Actions.

## Notes

- Upgrading from a Plus alpha or beta switches you to **Pixel Light** once. Pick any other theme afterwards and it
  stays picked. The 1.5 betas' *rocknixds-dark* and *rocknixds-light* skins are replaced by Pixel Dark and Pixel Light.
- Navigation sounds are switched on once by the installer; turn them off in *Sound settings* if you prefer silence.
- Uninstall at any time: `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/main/install.sh | sh -s -- --uninstall`.
