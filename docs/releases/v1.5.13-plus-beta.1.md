# ROCKNIXDS 1.5.13 beta 1 for the RG DS Plus: a new in-game menu
**A new in-game menu.** Clicking the left stick (L3) during a game now opens a menu in ROCKNIXDS Pixel's look
instead of DraStic's old one: big tiles, touch everywhere, save slots with pictures, and your latest achievements on
the top screen. The pixel font is easier to read too: **5** no longer looks like **S**.

> **Beta, RG DS Plus only.** Tested on an RG DS Plus. There is no RG DS (non-Plus) build of this yet: RG DS owners
> stay on **[v1.5.12](https://github.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/releases/tag/v1.5.12)**. Stable Plus users are not
> offered this beta; it comes through the beta channel only (see below).

## Install

On an RG DS Plus with ROCKNIXDS 1.5 or newer: **Updates & downloads > ROCKNIXDS**, set the channel to **beta**,
then update. Over ssh (`root`, password `rocknix`):

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/main/install.sh | RGDS_BRANCH=beta sh
```

Back to stable: set the channel to **stable** in the same menu and update; that installs 1.5.12 again.

## 🎮 The in-game menu

Press **L3** (or the mode key) while a game runs. The game, its sound and its clock stop until you close the menu.
Use the D-pad, the left stick or **touch** (DraStic's menu ignored the touchscreen). **A** picks, **B** goes back,
**L3** or **Start** closes it.

- **Resume.**
- **Save** and **Load**: 8 slots, each with a picture of the game as it was saved and when. The top screen shows the
  slot you're on, large. Replacing a save asks first. Saves made in DraStic's old menu show up too (slots 1–8 are
  DraStic's slots 0–7).
- **Undo last load** (**Y** on the Load page): every load first keeps the game as it was, so loading the wrong
  slot never costs you progress.
- **Quick settings**: volume and brightness (both screens), which change right away, and **Microphone**.
- **DraStic menu**: DraStic's original menu, for cheats, controls and the firmware settings.
- **Quit game**: asks first, then keeps your spot like the exit hotkey (with **resume on quit** on).

**Top screen**: the game, this session's play time, your last save and your **latest RetroAchievements** with their
badges and the progress bar. The server's "Warning: Unknown Emulator" entry (it only means hardcore isn't available
with DraStic) is no longer listed or shown as an unlock.

### 🎤 Microphone

**Quick settings > Microphone** shows a live meter of the handheld's mic: blow on it and the bar climbs; past the red
line, the game counts it as a blow ("Heard!"). The line follows the *microphone sensitivity* setting in ES's DS
options. With it **off** (the default), the game doesn't hear the real mic at all, and the page says so. **A** there blows
for you: the menu closes and the game hears 3 seconds of blowing, for games that ask you to blow (candles, pinwheels)
when the real mic doesn't do it.

## 🔤 An easier pixel font

Pixelify Sans drew its **5** like an **S**, so "10/05" read as "10/0S" ([#34](https://github.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/issues/34)).
Its **2** and **Z** were near twins, and **B** looked like **G** ("Pokémon Glack"). Those four are redrawn on the
font's own pixel grid with the same widths, so nothing moves; everything else in the font is unchanged. It applies to
the whole ROCKNIXDS Pixel menu and to the in-game menu.

## 🔔 Pop-ups in the same style

Achievement unlocks, the game's achievement summary, the volume indicator and the menu's messages ("Saved to slot
3") now use ROCKNIXDS Pixel's look: the dark dotted card with a light border, framed badges, the pixel font, and the
theme's gold, cyan and red.

## Other fixes

- The exit hotkey works while the in-game menu is open: it closes the menu, saves your spot and quits.
- DraStic's save/load buttons, pressed for you (resume on quit, the menu): a second press while the first was still
  held could leave the first button held down.

## Known limits

- **Fast forward** isn't in the menu: DraStic's fast forward has no button by default, and it couldn't be checked
  under SuperDrastic's frame pacing.
- The **Microphone** page's "Blow" was checked to reach DraStic, not yet in a game's blowing scene: reports welcome.
- Want DraStic's old menu back? Ask in an issue; SuperDrastic can switch the new one off (`DSFLIP_MENU=0`).
