# ROCKNIXDS 1.6 (draft)
**A game that gets stuck no longer needs a restart, the in-game menu comes to the RG DS, and the menu is back about a
second after you quit.** 1.6 is one release for both handhelds. It brings the RG DS everything since 1.5.12:
- fast switching for everyone;
- a fresh card that opens on the DS;
- the recommended settings as the defaults;
- an easier pixel font;
- the in-game menu that RG DS Plus testers have had since 1.5.13 beta 1;
- fixes for the reports since then.

> **Draft, not released.** The RG DS line has not been run on an RG DS since 1.5.8. Everything below is built and
> checked on a PC (what that means is under *What was tested*). The RG DS Plus line got the same changes and is
> partly tested on a Plus.
>
> **RG DS Plus owners:** your release is **v1.6-plus**.

## Install or upgrade

From 1.5: **Updates & downloads > ROCKNIXDS** in the menu. From anything older, ssh into the handheld as `root`
(password `rocknix`) and run:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/main/install.sh | sh
```

## 🛟 A stuck game never needs a restart again

Once, right after a game loaded its resume state, the game stopped drawing and nothing ended it. With *resume on
quit* on, the exit hotkey only asks the game to save at its next frame, and that frame never came. The handheld had
to be restarted. Now:
- **5 s without a picture** (outside DraStic's own menu and the in-game menu): a red card says *The game stopped
  responding*. The exit hotkey then takes you to the menu at once, and the log records what every part of the game
  was doing.
- **20 s without a picture**: the game is closed, and the menu says why.
- **The exit hotkey always ends the game.** A second press, or a game that doesn't take the save within 3 seconds,
  quits at once (without a resume state).
- **A game frozen solid** (even its display engine stuck) is ended from outside after 15 s, with the same message.
- The likely cause is fixed on the RG DS Plus, where the engine's CPU placement could leave a new DraStic thread
  unable to run. The RG DS never had that placement.

## 🎮 The in-game menu, on the RG DS too

Clicking the left stick (L3) during a game opens a menu in ROCKNIXDS Pixel's look instead of DraStic's old one:
- Resume;
- Save and Load, with eight slots, a picture in each, and *Undo last load*;
- Quick settings: volume, brightness, a live microphone meter and *Blow*;
- DraStic's own menu;
- Quit.

The top screen shows the paused game, this session's play time, the last save and your latest achievements.
Pop-ups (achievements, volume, saves) are in the same style. It was designed at the RG DS's 640×480, so it is drawn
at its native size here (the Plus scales it up), with its pixel text on the font's grid so the letters are sharp. [Screenshots from an RG DS
Plus](https://github.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/releases/tag/v1.5.13-plus-beta.1).

## ⏱️ Back to the menu in about a second, and it looks clean

Up to 1.5.12 a DS game stopped EmulationStation, and quitting started it again. That start reads every system's game
list, so the wait after a game grew with your library. Now:
- The menu stays up during DS games and is back about a second after you quit, whatever the size of your library.
- Other emulators behave as on stock ROCKNIX.
- Between the menu and the game the screens are plain black: no terminal cursor, and no leftover frame of the start
  animation.
- The menu appears only once it is drawing again.
- `/storage/.config/drastic/dsflip/fast-switch off` (over ssh) goes back to the old way.

## 🗂️ A fresh card opens on the DS

With no DS games yet, EmulationStation used to drop the DS. The menu then opened on Music Player and two empty
collections, and their placeholder hung the ready screen when tapped. Now:
- The DS stays listed and says **No games yet / Copy .nds files to roms/nds**.
- Collections say *Nothing here yet*.
- Collection names are capitalised on ROCKNIXDS Pixel's shelf (*Favorites*, not *favorites*).

## ⚙️ The best settings are the defaults

*Auto* is the best choice everywhere:
- the **Gengis Engine** renderer: the same picture as DraStic's renderer with about 12% less CPU work;
- the DS's own texture filter;
- the balanced power profile;
- threaded 3D, which the installer switches on where it was never set.

A player who picked *DraStic* keeps it.

## 🏆 RetroAchievements without a connection

A login that gets no answer (no Wi-Fi yet, a DNS hiccup, a busy server) no longer deletes your saved login. It is
tried again in the background: after 15 s, 30 s and a minute, then every 2 minutes. You see one pop-up, *no
connection*, instead of *login failed* with nothing after it. The log names the network error and the server's
answer.

## 🧠 The menu stays light

Once, on an RG DS Plus, EmulationStation grew to 713 MB until the system closed it, and the handheld didn't answer
for minutes. The likely cause is found and fixed, on both handhelds. With a menu open over a ROCKNIXDS Pixel game list
(game options, settings, a scrape), the theme asked for the same pictures again on every frame and kept every copy,
for as long as the menu stayed up. Tested on a PC: 43 MB more after 120 frames at the RG DS's size before the fix, flat
after it. 1.6 also makes sure nothing like it can take the handheld down again:
- The Pixel theme's picture cache has a size limit.
- Image memory goes back to the system when it is freed.
- The menu's memory is written to `es-mem.log` as it changes.
- If it ever passes half the handheld's memory while no game runs, the menu restarts by itself.
- Two quick launch requests start the game once, not twice.

## 🔤 An easier pixel font

In ROCKNIXDS Pixel's font, **5** no longer looks like **S**, **2** and **Z** are told apart, and **B** no longer
reads as **G** (issue #34).

## 🧩 From your reports

- **#36, #37:** the DS settings disappeared, and Tools and Music Player showed up in the system settings, when the
  options file was left broken. The file is now checked before it is replaced, and repaired or rebuilt from
  ROCKNIX's.
- **#31:** zipped DS games get their RetroAchievements id from the background job too (in-game achievements worked
  since 1.5.2).
- **#26:** the in-game menu's *Blow* presses the fake microphone however `drastic.cfg` binds it. A real blow is still
  being looked into: see *Known issues*.
- **#32, #34, #35:** *View Game Media*, the Save State Manager and *Manual Scrape* fit on the bottom screen instead of
  spreading over both.
  - A picture opens whole; L/R zoom and the D-pad moves it. The zoom view of *View Game Media*, always empty
    before, shows the picture.
  - The Save State Manager's title, *Start new game* and the slots are together on the bottom screen.
  - *Manual Scrape* shows the publisher, genre and release date instead of a letter or two.
- **#44:** *UI settings > ROM icon on cartridges (DS)* (ROCKNIXDS Pixel, off by default) shows a DS game's own icon,
  from its ROM, on the bottom screen's cartridges instead of the box art. Zipped games too.
- **#27:** ROCKNIXDS Pixel's game list takes touch: drag along its progress bar to scroll (the current letter rides
  above it) or tap the bar to jump, and tap the ◀ ▶ arrows by the names at the top.

## 📶 Online play is not in 1.6

The 1.5.13 test builds had a *wfc dns* option for Nintendo WFC replacement servers. It doesn't get past the game's
own Wi-Fi setup yet, so 1.6 neither shows it nor runs it. The work continues separately.

## Known issues

- **A real blow into the microphone may do nothing** (issue #26): the speaker reaches the microphone as loud as a
  blow, and the echo gate then holds its bar above any blow. The in-game menu's *Blow* is the way around it for now.
- **Japanese and Chinese game names** show as empty boxes in the in-game menu (the pixel font has no such letters).
- **RetroAchievements:** softcore only.
- **Heavy stretches at 2× can still drop frames** in some 3D games.
- **Front-end music** plays only files you add: copy `.mp3` or `.ogg` files to `roms` > `music` (ROCKNIX ships none;
  issue #43).
