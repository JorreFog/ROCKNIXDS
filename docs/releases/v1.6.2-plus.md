# ROCKNIXDS 1.6.2 for the RG DS Plus
**The ROCKNIXDS Store, a faster 3×, clocks that follow the power profile, and Döda Kvarter 0.3.** This is private test
build 7, released as it was tested.

## Install or upgrade

From 1.6 or 1.5: **Updates & downloads > ROCKNIXDS** in the menu. From anything else, ssh into the handheld as `root`
(password `rocknix`) and run:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/main/install.sh | sh
```

New SD card: the 1.6 image on the [v1.6-plus release](https://github.com/JorreFog/ROCKNIXDS/releases/tag/v1.6-plus),
then update from the menu.

## 🛍️ The ROCKNIXDS Store

A **Store** tile on the menu's shelf installs, updates and removes games and apps without waiting for a ROCKNIXDS
release: Döda Kvarter, ROCKNIXDS Bank & Trade, and **eReaderDS** by casualducko, a two-page ebook reader for the RG DS
Plus (installed from its developer's own releases, with their permission; books stay in `roms/ebook`).

- An **Updates** tab lists every installed app with a newer version, with what's new on the top screen. A updates
  one, Y updates them all.
- A check 6 minutes after boot and then every 6 hours tells the menu when an update is out (it follows the "check for
  updates automatically" switch).
- Every download is checked against its checksum; with none, nothing is installed. Saves and settings stay.

## 🎮 Nintendo DS

- **SuperDrastic 0.5.0-beta.1-rocknixds.9-test.6: a faster 3×.** The Gengis Engine with the cache work (prefetching
  in the pixel kernels, drawing straight into the display's buffers, no copy of a 3D frame that didn't change). Faster
  at 3× in every game tried (HeartGold 10% less CPU a frame, Mario Kart DS 4%), 2× unchanged and still exact. 3× now
  honours *disable edge marking* and draws the neighbouring polygons of issue #47 right. 3× also waits a little longer
  for a frame, which takes repeated frames from about one a second to a third.
- **The clocks follow the power profile.** The CPU governor holds the profile's bound from the start, and only goes
  past it (or remembers a clock as too slow) when the game really is CPU-bound. Before, a game could sit at 1992 MHz
  on any profile. With a shader the GPU runs at full clock only on the performance profile; balanced and battery let
  it scale from 400 MHz (ds-fsr keeps the full clock).

## Fixes

- **File Manager and PortMaster take the gamepad every time.** After a tap on the bottom screen they could ignore every
  button. (PortMaster still thinks the Plus is a TrimUI Brick; that's PortMaster's own list.)
- **Vertical arcade games no longer blank the bottom screen.** RetroArch's vertical-game check stacked the panels and
  nothing put them back until a reboot; the menu now puts both panels and the touchscreen back whenever a game ends.

## 🧟 Döda Kvarter 0.3

- **Kert Barlsson on the radio:** an old businessman broadcasting from a warehouse talks you through the story as you
  play, in English and Swedish (Settings > Radio turns him off).
- **Music:** songs for the title, the play, the bosses and game over.
- "Up to date" instead of "offline" in Game updates when there's no newer game.

## For testers

Diagnostics stay in this release: each DS game's log has the audio's state at its start (uptime, suspends, PipeWire's
clock), and the last five RetroArch launches are recorded in `/storage/.config/rocknixds/ra-launch/`. If audio
crackles after the handheld has been on for hours, or a game opens with the wrong aspect ratio, send those.
