Döda Kvarter, the zombie roguelike for both screens of the RG DS and RG DS Plus (ROCKNIXDS).

## What's new in 0.3

- **Kert Barlsson on the radio.** You wake up in a world the forces of evil have taken: the dead walk, and Mörkret has
  woken the old ones from the stories, eight Väktare that hold the land. Kert, an old businessman broadcasting from a
  warehouse, talks you through taking it back, district by district, Väktare by Väktare. His box comes up on the top
  screen now and then with the story, a word when you need it, and between rounds a tip. *Settings > Radio (Kert)*
  turns him off. How to play opens with the story.
- **Music**: new songs for the title, under the play, for the boss fights and for game over.

## Install

**On the handheld:** the game updates itself: start it with Wi-Fi on, and the title says a new version is out; then
**Settings > Game updates**. Or over ssh, as root: `/storage/.config/rocknixds/dodakvarter/update.sh install`. High
scores and settings stay.

Updating the game this way needs ROCKNIXDS 1.6.1 or later; on 1.6, update ROCKNIXDS first (**Updates & downloads >
ROCKNIXDS**). This is a pre-release on purpose: ROCKNIXDS's own updater only takes ROCKNIXDS's releases.
